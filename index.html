    How to use

    1. Squad page: paste names, positions (first = 1st choice) and minutes.
    2. Hit Auto plan. It chooses the block length that can deliver your minutes, balances every player's minutes first, then fits positions.
    3. Pitch page: tap a block tab, then drag bench → pitch to sub on, pitch → bench to sub off, slot → slot to swap.
    4. A change affects only that block.
