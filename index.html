    How to use

    1. Paste your squad, positions in preference order, optional preferred minutes.
    2. Leave players on auto or nudge their minutes with −/+ in 10′ steps; the bar shows whether the totals add up.
    3. Pick side / shape / match length at the top.
    4. Hit Auto plan for a full game, or Reset plan and drag players on yourself.
    5. Use the tabs under the pitch to jump to any block. Drag bench → pitch to sub on, pitch → bench to sub off, slot → slot to swap. On touch: tap a player, then tap the destination.
    6. A change affects only that block unless you tick Ripple forward. The toast tells you the new total and how far over/under the player's allocation it is.
