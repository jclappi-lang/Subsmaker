    How to use

    1. Paste your squad (positions + target minutes).
    2. Pick side / shape / match length.
    3. Hit Auto plan for a full game, or Reset plan and drag players on yourself.
    4. Click a snapshot chip or move the clock slider to edit that part of the match. Drag bench → pitch to sub on, pitch → bench to sub off, slot → slot to swap. On touch: tap a player, then tap the destination.
    5. Watch the minute columns and the Checks list — every drop tells you the before → after minutes.
