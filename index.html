    How to use

    1. Paste your squad (positions, optional preferred minutes).
    2. Adjust needed minutes with the −/+ steppers — the bar shows whether they add up to the pitch minutes available.
    3. Pick side / shape / match length at the top.
    4. Hit Auto plan for a full game, or Reset plan and drag players on yourself.
    5. Click a snapshot or move the clock slider to edit that part of the match. Drag bench → pitch to sub on, pitch → bench to sub off, slot → slot to swap. On touch: tap a player, then tap the destination.
    6. Every drop tells you the before → after minutes.
